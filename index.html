Chaotic Playground

Fractals from randomness

An interactive chaos game visualization. Pick vertices, choose a ratio, and watch structure emerge from pure randomness. The classic Sierpinski triangle is just the beginning.

  1. Place vertices on the canvas
  2. Pick a random vertex each step
  3. Move a fraction of the distance toward it
  4. Mark the spot. Repeat forever.
